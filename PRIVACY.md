# OpenMob Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-07-21**

OpenMob is an open-source app for controlling your own devices through the
OpenMob engine running on your own computer.

## What we collect

Nothing. OpenMob collects no personal data, no usage data, and no device
identifiers.

## How the app communicates

- The app connects **only** to the OpenMob engine that **you** run on a
  computer on **your own local network (LAN)**. You enter its address
  yourself.
- No traffic is sent to us or to any third party. There are no analytics,
  no crash reporting SDKs, no ads, and no third-party services of any kind.
- Screen images and input events from your devices travel directly between
  the app and your engine over your LAN and are not stored by the app beyond
  what is needed to display them.

## Demo mode

Demo mode runs entirely on your phone with simulated data. It makes no
network connections at all.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published in the
OpenMob source repository with a new effective date.
